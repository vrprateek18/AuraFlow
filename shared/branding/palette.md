# AuraFlow Visual Design System & Brand Palette

AuraFlow follows a premium, futuristic yet minimal design language inspired by touchless fluid motion and modern desktop operating systems (macOS Sonoma/Sequoia & Windows 11).

---

##  typography

* **Primary Sans-Serif:** `'Segoe UI'`, `'Inter'`, system `-apple-system`, `SF Pro Display`, sans-serif.
* **Monospaced Numbers / Telemetry:** `'Consolas'`, `'SF Mono'`, `'Menlo'`, monospace.

## 💎 Iconography & Emblem

The **AuraFlow Emblem** represents:
1. **Concentric Motion Ripples:** Radiating outward from the center, conveying soundless acoustic/optical waves.
2. **Dynamic Cursor Trajectory:** A curved gradient arc tracing natural hand acceleration.
3. **Pulsing Core Node:** The active tracking anchor point at the center.
