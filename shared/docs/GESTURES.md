# AuraFlow — Cross-Platform Gesture Cheatsheet

AuraFlow allows natural, touchless interaction using your hand in air in front of your webcam.

---

## 🖐️ Core Gestures

| Action | Platform | Gesture | Detailed Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Move Pointer** | Windows & macOS | **Point Index Finger** | Extend index finger and move smoothly. The cursor mirrors your index fingertip with 1-Euro adaptive velocity smoothing. |
| **Left Click** | Windows & macOS | **Pinch Thumb + Index** | Tap thumb and index fingertip together quickly and release. Triggers an instantaneous, crisp left mouse click. |
| **Right Click** | Windows | **Pinch Thumb + Middle** | Tap thumb and middle fingertip together and release. Triggers an instant context menu click. |
| **Smooth Scroll** | Windows & macOS | **Two Fingers Glide** | Extend index and middle fingers together side-by-side. Glide your hand up or down to scroll documents and web pages. |
| **Drag & Drop** | Windows & macOS | **Pinch & Hold** | Pinch thumb and index together firmly and hold for >250ms. Move hand to drag items or windows, then open hand to drop. |
| **Space Switch** | macOS | **Horizontal Palm Swipe** | Sweep open palm horizontally across the frame to switch full-screen macOS desktops (`Ctrl + Arrow`) or apps (`Cmd + Tab`). |
| **Radial Menu** | macOS & Windows | **Open Palm Steady** | Hold 5-finger open palm steady for ~0.5s to summon radial 3x3 quick action shortcut palette. |
| **Emergency Stop** | Windows & macOS | **Press F8 Hotkey** | Instantly suspends gesture injection, safely releases all held mouse buttons, and yields priority to physical mouse. |

---

## 💡 Best Practices for Reliable Tracking

1. **Camera Framing:** Position webcam so your hand is in the center 60% of the frame, approximately 1.5 to 2.5 feet (45–75 cm) away from the lens.
2. **Lighting:** Front-facing or ambient room lighting works best. Avoid bright windows or lamps directly behind your head.
3. **Rest Position:** When not controlling the computer, lower your hand or close your fist to automatically disengage the tracking clutch.
